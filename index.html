About Us

A Truly Relaxing, Authentic Environment

Welcome to a space where Eastern traditions meet modern Western culture. We built Smoky Ocean as the perfect place to slow down, unwind and enjoy real time with the people around you.

Every hookah here is treated as a small work of art — from the tobacco selection to the coal, the cleanliness of the process and the final presentation. Pair it with a cocktail from our bar, pick a board game, or grab a controller.
